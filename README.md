# Revenue & Profitability Analysis Dashboards

A Power BI dashboard developed to analyze revenue, profitability, costs, and overall business performance. The dashboard provides executive-level KPIs and detailed insights into revenue trends, profit margins, discount impacts, product performance, customer segments, and regional sales performance.

---

## Dashboard Previews

### Revenue & Profitability Overview

![Dashboard 1](revenue_dashboard_1.png)

This dashboard focuses on product profitability and revenue performance. It highlights key revenue KPIs such as Revenue YTD, Revenue MTD, and Revenue Last 3 Months, while providing detailed analysis of Revenue and Profit Margin by Product. Users can compare weekend and weekday sales performance, evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability, and identify top-performing and underperforming products through interactive visualizations and filtering capabilities.

---

### Financial Performance Analysis

![Dashboard 2](revenue_dashboard_2.png)

This dashboard provides an executive view of financial performance across different business dimensions. It includes key metrics such as Total Revenue, COGS, and Profit Margin, along with trend analysis over time. Users can explore financial performance by Country, Product, and Customer Segment to identify revenue drivers, compare profitability across markets, and monitor overall business growth through interactive and dynamic reporting.

## Business Value

The dashboards help stakeholders monitor financial performance, evaluate product profitability, assess the impact of discounts, compare regional performance, and identify opportunities for improving revenue and profit margins. The solution supports data-driven decision-making through interactive and visually engaging analytics.
